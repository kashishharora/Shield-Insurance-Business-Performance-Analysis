# Shield Insurance: Business Performance & Customer Analytics

An interactive 3-page Power BI dashboard built for a client pilot project at **Shield Insurance**, as part of the **Codebasics Virtual Internship**.

## Business Problem
Shield Insurance wanted to understand its customer base, track business performance, and identify opportunities for growth. The client asked for a pilot dashboard to monitor customers, revenue and policies, and to break them down by age group, city and sales mode.

## Dashboard Pages
1. **Business Performance Overview:** total customers, total revenue, daily revenue growth, daily customer growth, month-over-month policy change, a revenue/customer trend chart with a switch, and a city-wise table
2. **Sales Mode Analysis:** customer and revenue split by sales mode, and monthly revenue trend by sales mode
3. **Age Group Analysis:** customers by age group, sales mode and policy, and expected settlement % by age group

Filters are available for sales mode, age group, city, month and policy ID.

## Key Insights
- **Overall:** 26.8K customers and 989.25M total revenue
- **Delhi NCR** is the top city, with 401.57M revenue and 11.0K customers (roughly 40% of total revenue)
- The **36-45 age group** has the largest customer base (around 10K) and the highest revenue
- **Offline Agent** is the leading sales mode, bringing about 55% of both customers and revenue
- **Expected settlement %** is highest for older age groups and lowest for the 18-25 group

## Tools Used
- **Power BI** for the interactive dashboard
- **Power Query** for data preparation
- **DAX** for measures and KPIs
